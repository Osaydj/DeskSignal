# Northstar Office Co. 2025 Commercial Strategy

This fictional strategy document summarizes the business focus for each quarter of 2025.

## First-quarter focus

The first quarter prioritizes returning-customer retention and stable pricing. Marketing should emphasize dependable workspace essentials rather than large discounts.

## Second-quarter focus

The second quarter centers on home-office refresh campaigns. Featured products include the HD Webcam and Ergonomic Chair, with business-customer outreach for furniture orders. Most second-quarter campaigns are scheduled to end by June 30.

## Third-quarter focus

The third quarter introduces the Portable Monitor and shifts Electronics advertising toward portable-work products. During July and August, the plan reallocates a meaningful share of homepage space and paid media from the HD Webcam and Wireless Mouse to the Portable Monitor, Mechanical Keyboard, and USB-C Hub. This is a planned attention shift, not a conclusion about why any individual transaction changed.

## Fourth-quarter focus

The fourth quarter prioritizes giftable technology, premium audio, and business budget completion. Noise-Canceling Headphones and selected Electronics bundles receive increased promotional support, while furniture campaigns remain margin-conscious.

## Measurement approach

Quarterly reviews should compare revenue, profit, profit margin, units, and transactions. Strategy timing may help form an investigation hypothesis, but analysts must use calculated sales data to verify whether performance changed during the same period.
