# Northstar Office Co. Sales and Discount Policy

This fictional policy describes how the sample company approves promotions and protects margins.

## Standard discount limits

Sales representatives may approve discounts up to ten percent for standard orders. Larger discounts require approval from the commercial director. Discounts must never reduce an order below the category margin floor unless the finance team records a temporary exception.

## Promotion scheduling

Major promotions should have a written start date, end date, target product, and target region. When a promotion ends, the product returns to its normal price and normal website placement unless a strategy document says otherwise. Performance reviews should compare the promotional period with the period immediately after it.

## Margin protection

The normal company-wide margin target is thirty-five percent. Office Furniture may operate as low as thirty percent because shipping and handling costs are higher. If a category remains below its floor for two monthly reviews, the pricing team should investigate discounts, freight costs, and product mix.

## Evidence limitations

A scheduled promotion can coincide with a sales increase or decline, but the schedule alone does not prove causation. Analysts should combine policy documents with transaction calculations and describe document events as possible contributors.
