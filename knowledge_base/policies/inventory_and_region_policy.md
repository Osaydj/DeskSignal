# Northstar Office Co. Inventory and Regional Policy

This fictional policy explains how inventory constraints and regional allocations are handled.

## Low-stock response

When expected inventory coverage falls below three weeks, the operations team may reduce paid advertising, remove featured placement, or restrict bulk orders until supply recovers. The product remains available, but lower visibility can affect sales while the restriction is active.

## Regional allocation

Inventory is normally allocated using recent demand from the North, South, East, West, and Central regions. A temporary allocation change must name the affected product, regions, and review date. Regional performance differences may reflect demand, inventory availability, marketing, or other factors not included in the sales dataset.

## Back-order communication

Products expected to be delayed by more than ten business days should not be included in a new promotion. Customer-facing teams should use the approved availability message and avoid promising a delivery date that operations has not confirmed.

## Analysis guidance

An inventory note is supporting context rather than proof that inventory caused a measured sales movement. Analysts should look for matching dates, products, and regions before treating the note as relevant.
