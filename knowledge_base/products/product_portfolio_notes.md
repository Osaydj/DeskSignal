# Northstar Office Co. Product Portfolio Notes

These fictional product notes document launches, positioning, and temporary commercial decisions for the sample product catalog.

## HD Webcam

The HD Webcam was featured in the Spring Video Setup promotion through June 30, 2025. The promotion included homepage placement and a small bundle discount. Beginning July 1, the homepage position and most paid media budget moved to the Portable Monitor launch. The webcam remained available at its standard price, and the marketing team planned to review its performance in September.

## Portable Monitor

The Portable Monitor launched nationally on July 1, 2025. It received the main Electronics homepage position during July and August, supported by a launch bundle with the USB-C Hub. The launch plan expected some customers who were considering other Electronics accessories to shift attention toward the new monitor.

## Ergonomic Chair

The Ergonomic Chair was included in a business-customer bulk-order campaign during May and June 2025. That campaign ended June 30. July inventory was intentionally held closer to normal levels while the operations team reviewed freight capacity for large furniture orders.

## Noise-Canceling Headphones

Noise-Canceling Headphones are positioned as a premium focus product. The 2025 plan reserves the largest promotion for the holiday period, with smaller testing campaigns allowed earlier in the year.

## Standing Desk

The Standing Desk is aimed mainly at business customers and home-office buyers. Discounts are limited because freight costs are high, so performance should be reviewed using both revenue and profit margin rather than revenue alone.

## Everyday accessories

The Wireless Mouse, Mechanical Keyboard, Laptop Stand, Desk Lamp, Cable Organizer, Travel Tech Case, and USB-C Hub are normally sold without dedicated national campaigns. They may appear in bundles supporting a larger product launch.
